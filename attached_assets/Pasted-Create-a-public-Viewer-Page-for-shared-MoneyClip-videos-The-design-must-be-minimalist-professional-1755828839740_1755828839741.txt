Create a public Viewer Page for shared MoneyClip videos. The design must be minimalist, professional, and consistent with fintech SaaS products for advisors. The page enforces a Terms gate before playback, applies advisor branding, and provides clear viewer controls and client engagement features.

Layout & Branding

Header (white background):

Left: Advisor logo (from advisor branding settings).

Right: Client name (from advisor entry on Preview/Save).

Page background:

Advisor’s primary brand color (from branding settings).

Below header (main video area):

Embedded video player.

Player supports playback speed controls (0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, 2x).

Player supports captions toggle (captions auto-generated during recording).

Advisor’s profile picture or webcam video bubble appears on video overlay (location consistent with what advisor chose during recording).

Below video player:

Compliments section:

Row of pre-made compliments and emojis (e.g., 👍, 👏, “This was helpful,” “Thanks for explaining”).

One-click send; viewer feedback stored.

Video title & description (from AI-generated + advisor edits).

Calls to action (CTAs):

Email Advisor (mailto link).

Call Advisor (tel link).

Schedule Follow-Up (advisor’s scheduling link from branding page, e.g., Calendly).

Footer (minimal):

Reserved for future disclosures or legal links.

Terms Gate

Before playback, the viewer must accept a Terms & Disclosure gate.

Disclosure text is defined and editable by the advisor in Settings.

Modal overlay appears on first visit; must be accepted to unlock video.

Consent stored per session (so the viewer doesn’t need to re-accept repeatedly).

Viewer Controls

Playback speed options: 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, 2x.

Captions toggle: Captions on/off.

Full-screen mode.

Volume control.

Responsive layout: Works smoothly on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Behavior

Branding: Logo, profile picture, and primary color load from advisor’s Branding page.

Client name: Displays in header (top right).

Compliments: Viewer can click to send; feedback recorded and tied to that video.

CTAs: Open email, call, or scheduling link in default apps/browser.

Captions: Show at bottom of video if enabled; viewer can toggle off.

Webcam/profile photo: Displayed as small PiP bubble overlay in a corner of the player.

Compliance & Privacy

Audit log records: VIEWER_ACCEPTED_TERMS, VIDEO_PLAYED, VIDEO_PAUSED, VIDEO_COMPLETED, CAPTIONS_ON/OFF, SPEED_CHANGED, COMPLIMENT_SENT.

Store only minimal viewer interaction data; do not capture PII beyond what is strictly needed (no raw IPs).

Terms gate ensures disclosure acceptance before video can play.

Acceptance Criteria

On visiting the link, I see advisor logo (top left), client name (top right), and advisor brand color as background.

A Terms gate modal appears. I cannot play until I accept the advisor’s disclosure.

Once accepted, video player appears with controls for play, pause, volume, full-screen, speed, and captions.

Advisor’s profile photo or webcam bubble appears in the player.

Below the video: compliment buttons + emojis, video title, description, and CTAs for email, call, and schedule.

Compliment clicks register feedback.

Captions appear on playback and can be toggled off by the viewer.

Speed control works as expected.

Page adapts cleanly to mobile.

Manual Test Steps

Open a shared video link → confirm Terms gate blocks playback. Accept terms → video unlocked.

Confirm advisor logo, client name, and background branding load correctly.

Start playback → confirm profile photo/webcam bubble shows.

Toggle captions on/off. Confirm captions display at bottom.

Change playback speed to 1.5x and 0.5x → confirm playback adjusts.

Send a compliment emoji → confirm it registers (e.g., shows a confirmation).

Click “Email Advisor” → opens default mail app.

Click “Call Advisor” → opens dialer.

Click “Schedule Follow-Up” → opens advisor’s scheduling link.

Resize browser to mobile width → confirm responsive layout.

No stack, framework, or library suggestions.
