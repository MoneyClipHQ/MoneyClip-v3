Prompt to Replit:
Create a minimalist Advisor Dashboard for MoneyClip. Keep the layout clean, fast to scan, and consistent with modern advisor fintech apps.

Goals:

Make recording the primary action.

Give quick access to the video library and scripted content.

Show the three most recent videos for fast resume.

Provide a simple account menu with branding, compliance, settings, and sign out.

Layout and IA:

Header:

Left: MoneyClip logo (clickable to Dashboard).

Right: Advisor’s name. Clicking opens a dropdown: Branding, Compliance, Settings, Sign out.

Primary navigation (top row under header): Two buttons: Video Library, Scripted Content.

Primary action (center-stage): Large “Record” button. Clear label and subtext like “Record your screen with voice.”

Recent items: A row showing the advisor’s three most recent videos as simple, clickable thumbnails or boxes. Each shows title, created/updated time, and a subtle play or open icon.

Empty state: If no recent videos, show a friendly message and a “Record your first video” button.

Behavior:

Clicking Record takes the advisor directly into the recording flow.

Clicking a recent video opens its detail page.

Video Library and Scripted Content lead to their respective pages.

Advisor name dropdown opens on click or Enter and closes on outside click or Escape.

Content and labels:

Clear, short labels: “Record,” “Video Library,” “Scripted Content,” “Branding,” “Compliance,” “Settings,” “Sign out.”

No jargon. Keep helper copy to one short line where needed.

Design requirements:

Minimalist, professional aesthetic suitable for advisor fintech.

Responsive on desktop and mobile.

Obvious visual priority on the Record button.

Consistent spacing, typography, and hover/focus states.

Accessibility:

All interactive elements are keyboard reachable in a logical order.

Visible focus states.

Dropdown can be opened and navigated with keyboard.

Acceptance criteria:

I see header with logo left and advisor name right.

Clicking the advisor name opens a dropdown with: Branding, Compliance, Settings, Sign out. Each item is selectable.

I see two top buttons: Video Library and Scripted Content.

A large center “Record” button is present and clearly the primary action.

I see the three most recent videos as clickable thumbnails or boxes with title and time info.

If there are no videos, I see an empty state with a call to start recording.

Page looks clean and works on desktop and mobile.

Manual test steps:

Open Dashboard. Confirm header, nav buttons, central Record, and recent videos appear.

Click Record. Confirm navigation into the recording flow.

Click a recent video. Confirm it opens the video detail page.

Open the advisor dropdown. Navigate with keyboard and select Settings.

Resize to mobile width. Confirm layout adapts and all controls are usable.

Test with zero videos. Confirm empty state and “Record your first video” path.

Guardrails:

Do not add extra widgets or marketing copy.

Keep text short; avoid clutter.

No stack or library choices in this prompt.
